About Bulk Image Resizer Pro

Bulk Image Resizer Pro is built by PuMita Tech as a fast, free, browser-based tool for resizing, compressing, and converting images in batches — without uploading a single file to a server.

Why we built this tool

Resizing a handful of images one at a time in a full photo editor is slow, and most online "bulk resize" services ask you to upload your files to their servers first, then wait for a download link. We wanted something faster and more private: a tool that runs entirely on your own device, processes images the moment you drop them in, and never asks you to hand your files over to anyone.

Bulk Image Resizer Pro uses the HTML5 canvas API built into every modern browser to redraw and re-encode images at the dimensions and quality you choose. There's no server-side processing involved at any point, which means the tool works quickly, works offline once the page has loaded, and keeps your images on your own machine throughout.

Who it's for

The tool is used by designers preparing assets at multiple sizes, online sellers standardizing product photography across a catalog, social media creators resizing batches of photos for different platforms, and anyone who simply has a folder of images that all need to be a certain size or file type.

Our approach

We keep the tool focused on one job — resizing, compressing, and converting images — and we try to do that job well rather than bolting on unrelated features. The interface is designed to be usable without a manual: drop your images in, adjust dimensions and quality with live feedback, and download the results individually or as a ZIP archive.

PuMita Tech maintains this tool and the small set of companion pages linked in the footer. If you have feedback or run into an issue, the contact page has the details.
